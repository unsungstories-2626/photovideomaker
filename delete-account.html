Delete your Photo Video Maker account

App: Photo Video Maker · Developer: CodeByRushi

You can delete your Photo Video Maker account and its data at any time.

Option 1 – In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to Profile › Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm (Google may ask you to choose your account again).
  3. Your account is deleted from our account system immediately.

Option 2 – Without the app

  1. Send an email to rushikeshkhode2626@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your email address, name, profile photo link, account ID and sign-in dates.
  • Not affected: photos, drafts and videos on your phone (they were never uploaded), and files you saved to your gallery. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove local data.
  • Subscription: deleting the account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in Play Store › Payments & subscriptions. Google keeps purchase records as required by law.
