Privacy Policy

Photo Video Maker – Photo to Video Editor (Android app, package com.codebyrushi.photovideomaker)
Developer: CodeByRushi · Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Photo Video Maker app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies only to this app. By using the app you agree to this policy.

In short
  • Your photos, videos and music are processed only on your phone. The app never uploads them to us or to anyone else.
  • An account is optional. It uses "Sign in with Google" and is only needed to buy Premium.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can delete your account at any time, inside the app or by email.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Photos and videos you choose

  • You choose photos with Android's own Photo Picker. The app can only see the items you pick, never your whole gallery.
  • Picked photos are copied into the app's private storage so you can edit them and continue drafts later. Other apps cannot read this storage.

Music you choose

  • You can use the songs built into the app or pick a song from your phone with the system picker. A picked song is copied into the draft on your phone.

Your creations

  • Videos, collages and edited photos you save are written to your phone's gallery (Movies/PhotoVideoMaker and Pictures/PhotoVideoMaker). They stay there even if you uninstall the app.
  • The "My Creations" list and in-app notifications (for example "Video saved") are created on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Settings

  • Settings such as theme, haptic feedback and export alerts are stored on your phone.

We (the developer) have no access to any of this information.

2. Your account (optional – Sign in with Google)

The only way to sign in is "Sign in with Google". Accounts are managed with Google Firebase Authentication.

  • What we receive from Google: your name, email address and profile photo link. We never see your Google password.
  • What is stored (on Firebase servers): that information, a user ID, and account creation / last sign-in dates.
  • Why: to sign you in, show your profile in the app, and link a Premium subscription to exactly one account.
  • We do not send you marketing emails and do not use your account data for advertising.
  • Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Premium subscription (Google Play Billing)

  • Premium is sold only through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • When you buy, the app gives Google Play a code made from your account ID (a one-way hash, not your email). The app later asks Google Play which subscription belongs to that code, so one subscription stays with one account.
  • The app keeps a yes/no "Premium active" value on your phone.
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice: payments.google.com – Privacy Notice

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Users without Premium see full-screen ads (interstitial and rewarded interstitial ads) from Google AdMob. Premium users see no ads.

  • To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect: your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, Android version, language), approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads.
  • Depending on your choices and the law where you live, ads may be personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent using Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Background Remover (Google ML Kit)

  • The Background Remover in the photo editor uses Google ML Kit subject segmentation. It runs on your phone: your photos are not sent to Google or to us.
  • The model is downloaded and kept up to date by Google Play services, which may send Google limited technical information about the feature (performance and diagnostics, never your photos).

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state: for ads, Google sign-in and the subscription check. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and check the Premium subscription.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described in section 4.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): asked right before saving, only to write your creation to the gallery.
  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on export alerts in Settings.
  • Wake lock: keeps the screen on while a video is being exported.

The app does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS or phone permissions, and it cannot read your whole photo gallery.

8. How we share information

We share information only as described above:

  • with service providers that process it for us: Google Firebase (accounts), Google Play (subscriptions), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play services (ML Kit);
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the app.

We do not sell your personal data and we never share your photos or videos with anyone.

9. Sharing your creations

When you tap Share, Android's share screen opens and you choose where the file goes. The app sends nothing by itself. What happens after that is covered by the privacy policy of the app you shared to.

10. Backup

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back up the app's settings to your own Google account. Your drafts and private photo copies are not included in cloud backup. A direct phone-to-phone transfer (cable or Wi-Fi) may copy your drafts to your new phone.

11. How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • On your phone: until you delete it. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove all private data. Files saved to your gallery stay until you delete them.
  • Your account: until you delete it. In the app go to Profile › Account › Delete account; this deletes your account from Firebase immediately. You can also ask us by email (section 15) and we will delete it within 7 days. More details: Delete your account.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Play Store › Payments & subscriptions.
  • Ad data and purchase records are kept by Google as described in Google's own policies.

12. Children

The app is meant for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Accounts are protected by Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication over encrypted connections. The app never handles your Google password. Your photos and drafts stay in Android's private app storage, protected by your phone's screen lock and encryption. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use industry-standard services to protect your information.

14. Your rights

You can view your account, sign out, and delete your account in the app at any time. You can also contact us to ask what data your account holds, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA) you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion, and withdrawing consent, and we will respect them. We will reply to requests within 30 days.
